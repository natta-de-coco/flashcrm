# WhatsApp marketing safety rules

FLAS treats a WhatsApp broadcast as marketing unless it is explicitly a utility or authentication message. Marketing must use an approved WhatsApp template and must only go to people who have actively opted in to receive WhatsApp marketing.

Before a recipient can enter a campaign, FLAS requires all of these:

- an approved template;
- recorded consent on the contact;
- a source for that consent, such as a form, checkout, written request, or consent capture date;
- no unsubscribe or manual suppression; and
- no prior marketing broadcast to that person in the previous three days.

The three-day limit is a FLAS anti-spam guardrail. It is not a claim that Meta permits a particular volume. The campaign screen must show excluded recipients and the reason for each exclusion, and it must offer a small test batch before a larger send.

Every outbound campaign recipient is recorded with its template, consent evidence, send result, and later delivery/read/reply status. A user who replies with an opt-out request must be suppressed before any later marketing selection. Support conversations and transactional updates still follow their own WhatsApp consent and 24-hour conversation-window rules.

FLAS must not import chat history from another company or another CRM. For a workspace's own historical conversations, use an explicit import with source ownership confirmation and label the result as imported history.

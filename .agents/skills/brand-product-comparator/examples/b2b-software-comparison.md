<h2>Where both products overlap</h2>

<p>
  It is important to be fair: both Flas CRM and Wati provide robust foundational tools for teams migrating off the standard WhatsApp Business App.
</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Official WhatsApp Cloud API Access</strong>: Both platforms connect via official Meta API routes, safeguarding your phone numbers against spam bans.</li>
  <li><strong>Multi-Agent Team Routing</strong>: Both allow multiple agents to reply simultaneously from the same WhatsApp phone number.</li>
  <li><strong>Automated Routing & Tags</strong>: Both support assigning conversations based on keywords, shift hours, or agent availability.</li>
</ul>

<p>
  If your only objective is having five people answer general customer questions from one phone number, either platform will function reliably. 
  The separation occurs when customer conversations become financial transactions.
</p>

<h2>Where Flas CRM is the better fit</h2>

<p>
  Flas CRM is built specifically for trading companies, professional service firms, and B2B wholesale operations where the person answering the message is also responsible for closing the sale.
</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Zero Context-Switching for Sales Documents</strong>: Your reps never leave the chat window to prepare quotes, track invoices, or log payments.</li>
  <li><strong>Integrated Pipeline Visibility</strong>: Sales managers can view deals by expected closing date and revenue, rather than guessing pipeline health from raw message volume.</li>
  <li><strong>Lower Total Tech Stack Cost</strong>: You do not need to pay for a separate CRM subscription and Zapier connector just to link your WhatsApp inbox to your deals.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Where Wati is the better fit (When NOT to choose Flas CRM)</h2>

<p>
  We build Flas CRM, but we believe in honest guidance. Wati is the stronger platform in several clear operational scenarios:
</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Large Omnichannel Support Teams</strong>: If you need to manage Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger, and WhatsApp in a unified ticketing queue primarily for customer care, Wati's mature inbox routing is purpose-built for that workload.</li>
  <li><strong>High-Volume Broadcast Marketing</strong>: If your primary use case is sending hundreds of thousands of promotional broadcasts with complex sequence funnels and click-to-WhatsApp ad attribution, Wati's campaign engine offers deeper broadcast tooling.</li>
  <li><strong>Existing Deep HubSpot / Salesforce Deployments</strong>: If your organization already has an entrenched enterprise CRM and only requires a dedicated WhatsApp bridge, Wati's integrations may fit your existing stack with less disruption.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Your 14-Day Trial Checklist</h2>

<p>
  Do not make a purchasing decision based on a vendor sales pitch. Put both systems through this 5-point trial checklist:
</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Simulate an incomplete lead</strong>: Submit a website inquiry without a phone number and test how easily your team recovers it.</li>
  <li><strong>Generate an urgent quotation</strong>: Measure the exact seconds it takes an agent to create and send a formatted quote to a test number.</li>
  <li><strong>Perform a silent shift handoff</strong>: Have Agent B take over a negotiation from Agent A without any verbal briefing.</li>
  <li><strong>Verify local tax rules</strong>: Ensure VAT numbers and legal company details format correctly on customer-facing PDFs.</li>
  <li><strong>Inspect mobile usability</strong>: Test whether sales reps can approve quotes or check deals from their phones while off-site.</li>
